# Theme credits

- `default` and `light` are ctui's own.
- The Monkeytype themes are listed in [MONKEYTYPE.md](MONKEYTYPE.md) (GPL-3.0).
- These use the colours of well-known palettes. `bg`, `fg`, `sub`, `main` and
  `error` are picked from the palette, and `lists` is the palette's accent
  colours. All the upstream projects are MIT-licensed.
